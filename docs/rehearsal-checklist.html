Rehearsal checklist

Print this or keep it open on another device. Do a full run-through at least once

in the real venue lighting before the first show.

The day before

iPhone settings (Settings app)

Setup in the box (about 15 minutes before)

Know your sounds

SoundMeaning
Three rising notesApp started
Two rising notesArmed (watching for cards)
Two falling notesDisarmed
One high blipCard captured
Soft low tick (every 2 s)Waiting for Gemini
Two bright notes, then speechThe answer
Low two-note buzz, then your stall lineSlow, failed, or unreadable

Earbud controls

PressDefault action
SingleArm / disarm
DoubleCapture now (skips the stillness check)
TripleRepeat last answer

You can change these in Earbud buttons.

Keyboard or clicker (optional)

A keyboard or Bluetooth page-turner paired to the phone works too. Space / Enter = single press,

→ / Page Down = double, ← / Page Up = triple. Letters: A arm/disarm, C capture,

R repeat, S show mode, K restart camera, Esc leave show mode. Turn it off under Keyboard & clicker.

8BitDo / other controllers: pair in iPhone Settings → Bluetooth, tap START, then under Keyboard & clicker → Your own buttons tap Learn next to each action and press the button you want.

Heat

  • Use SHOW MODE whenever you can. A black screen saves the most power.
  • Keep it disarmed until close to the start. Disarmed, it checks only once a second.
  • Lower Camera & crop → Camera frame rate to 10 fps if the phone gets warm.
  • If you can, leave a small air gap or vent in the box. Don't run it while charging inside a

closed box.

If something goes wrong on stage

What you hearWhat to do
Nothing after placing a cardDouble-press to force a capture
Stall lineGemini was slow or couldn't read it. Talk through it; a late answer may still come
Same answer as the previous cardTriple-press repeats the last answer; double-press to read the new card
Wrong answerCheck the Capture log afterwards to see what the camera saw
Picture frozen or black on the screenThe app restarts the camera by itself within about 5 seconds. You can also tap Camera ↻
