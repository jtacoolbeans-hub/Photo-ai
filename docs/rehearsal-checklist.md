# Rehearsal checklist

Print this or keep it open on another device. Do a full run-through at least once
in the real venue lighting before the first show.

## The day before

- [ ] Phone charged to 100%. Bring the charging cable and a battery pack.
- [ ] Earbuds charged.
- [ ] Open the app once on the venue Wi-Fi or mobile data, so you get the latest version.
- [ ] **Connection → Test connection** shows four ✅ lines.
- [ ] Print 10 practice cards in 12pt Times New Roman, like your manager's.

## iPhone settings (Settings app)

- [ ] **Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never** (backup in case the app's screen lock fails)
- [ ] **Battery → Low Power Mode → Off** (Low Power slows the camera and can let the screen sleep)
- [ ] **Focus → Do Not Disturb → On**, so no calls or notification sounds reach your earbud
- [ ] **Notifications → Announce Notifications → Off**
- [ ] **Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices**: download an "Enhanced" English voice, then pick it
      in the app under **Speech & sounds → Voice** (if it appears in the list). It sounds much more natural.
- [ ] **Safari → Camera → Allow** (so it doesn't ask every time)
- [ ] Close music apps. If another app is playing audio, the earbud buttons go to it instead.

## Setup in the box (about 15 minutes before)

- [ ] Earbuds in and connected. Tap **Test voice** in **Speech & sounds** and check you hear it.
- [ ] Open the app (from the Home Screen icon or Safari) and tap **START**. You hear three rising notes.
- [ ] Put the phone in the box. Place a practice card where the real cards will go.
- [ ] Check the camera view shows the card sharply. Tap **Draw crop box** and drag a box around the
      card area with a little margin, then tap **Done drawing**.
- [ ] Watch the meters:
  - **motion** should drop below the motion threshold (it turns green) when nothing is moving.
  - **sharpness** with a card in place should be well above **Minimum sharpness** (green). With
    the box empty it should be below. If not, adjust **Detection → Minimum sharpness**.
- [ ] **Arm** with a single earbud press. You hear two rising notes.
- [ ] Run 3–5 practice cards end to end. Check that each one:
  - plays a high **blip** (captured), then soft **ticks** (thinking), then a **chime** and the answer
  - is read only once, until you swap the card
- [ ] Try a **double press** (capture now) and a **triple press** (repeat last answer).
- [ ] Pick **Capture speed** under **Detection** (Fast is the default; Fastest reads the first sharp frame).
- [ ] Under **Speech & sounds → Cues**, turn off any cue you don't want to hear. (iPhone can't vibrate from a web page.)
- [ ] Tap **SHOW MODE**. The screen goes black (double-tap the screen to come back).
- [ ] Leave it running and armed. Check the phone isn't getting hot (see below).

## Know your sounds

| Sound | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Three rising notes | App started |
| Two rising notes | Armed (watching for cards) |
| Two falling notes | Disarmed |
| One high blip | Card captured |
| Soft low tick (every 2 s) | Waiting for Gemini |
| Two bright notes, then speech | The answer |
| Low two-note buzz, then your stall line | Slow, failed, or unreadable |

## Earbud controls

| Press | Default action |
|---|---|
| Single | Arm / disarm |
| Double | Capture now (skips the stillness check) |
| Triple | Repeat last answer |

You can change these in **Earbud buttons**.

## Keyboard or clicker (optional)

A keyboard or Bluetooth page-turner paired to the phone works too. **Space / Enter** = single press,
**→ / Page Down** = double, **← / Page Up** = triple. Letters: **A** arm/disarm, **C** capture,
**R** repeat, **S** show mode, **K** restart camera, **Esc** leave show mode. Turn it off under **Keyboard & clicker**.

## Heat

- Use **SHOW MODE** whenever you can. A black screen saves the most power.
- Keep it **disarmed** until close to the start. Disarmed, it checks only once a second.
- Lower **Camera & crop → Camera frame rate** to 10 fps if the phone gets warm.
- If you can, leave a small air gap or vent in the box. Don't run it while charging inside a
  closed box.

## If something goes wrong on stage

| What you hear | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nothing after placing a card | Double-press to force a capture |
| Stall line | Gemini was slow or couldn't read it. Talk through it; a late answer may still come |
| Same answer as the previous card | Triple-press repeats the last answer; double-press to read the new card |
| Wrong answer | Check the **Capture log** afterwards to see what the camera saw |
| Picture frozen or black on the screen | The app restarts the camera by itself within about 5 seconds. You can also tap **Camera ↻** |

## After the show

- [ ] Open **CAPTURE LOG**. Check each thumbnail, its sharpness, timing and reply.
- [ ] Tap **Export** to save the log as a file (Files app or AirDrop).
- [ ] Note any misreads, and adjust the crop, lighting or thresholds before next time.
